The graph, the scoring model, and the rules of the investigation are inspectable. Here is what ARIADNE does—and what its results mean.

01

One graph.

02

Recorded evidence.
Separate hypotheses.

Imported relationships describe records in a biological knowledge graph. A model score orders candidates for investigation. It is neither a clinical efficacy estimate nor a causal claim.

Known treatment relationships remain separate from the evidence graph. They supply evaluation labels and are never used as predictive edges.

03

Challenge the evidence.
Keep the model fixed.

Excluding a relationship family changes the available paths and recomputes graph features. The same fitted model scores the revised evidence.

This is an evidence sensitivity analysis. Removing an edge from the graph does not reproduce a knockout experiment or an intervention in a patient.

04

Useful answers.
Visible boundaries.

The live agent follows a bounded LangGraph workflow and read-only tools. It resolves the question, retrieves rankings and paths, and produces an evidence-grounded brief.

The published research edition uses recorded results and a deterministic guide. The interface always identifies which edition is running.

CERTIFIED WITHIN A DECLARED SCOPE

Challenge the ranking.
Keep the proof.

A shortlist audit asks how far a selected compound’s supported rank can fall when up to two recorded binding edges are withdrawn anywhere in the graph.

ONE RECORD · A RIVAL’S SCOREInspect the rival-change example ↗

Vascular cancer / Nifedipine. Selected after the full benchmark to illustrate why rival records matter; this is an illustration, not a separate validation set.

The audit starts with the unmodified graph, includes every compound and competing score, and keeps the fitted model fixed. Its exact envelope distinguishes a worse supported rank from losing every supporting path. A witness identifies the actual imported records that attain each bound.

Certificates identify the data, graph, model and declared audit arithmetic. Live replay rechecks the bound and separately recomputes witness states. In the research snapshot, certificates and verification results are recorded artifacts; no server replay runs in the browser.

These are finite, conditional model guarantees. The audit does not establish biological truth, clinical efficacy, source independence, or robustness beyond its stated operation and budget.

EVIDENCE CAN SHARE A DEPENDENCY

Audit groups together.
Carry the assumptions forward.

A record-level withdrawal budget does not cover a shared source or imported reference attached to many records. The group audit enumerates every allowed combination of up to two chosen provenance groups.

MARIMASTAT / RHEUMATOID ARTHRITISInspect the shared-source example ↗

This illustration uses actual imported record memberships. It is not an independent biological validation case.

The declared stress rule withdraws every binding record mentioning a selected group. Overlapping memberships are deduplicated. Source names and PubMed annotations are not interpreted as independent replications or verified study dependencies.

The conditional protection calculation finds the fewest additional groups whose withdrawal must be ruled out to remove every failing scenario. Reviewing a group does not establish that its evidence is true; the result is an explicit sensitivity condition, not an optimal experiment policy.

The downloadable review dossier includes the original shortlist, source witnesses, conditional assumptions and complete certificate identity. Recorded editions load exported certificates; the full application performs new audits and verification.

Interpretation comes first.

The graph is incomplete and reflects historical research priorities. Unobserved treatment links are not proven negatives. Source agreement, graph proximity, or a high model rank cannot establish that a compound is effective or safe. ARIADNE is a research and teaching application.
